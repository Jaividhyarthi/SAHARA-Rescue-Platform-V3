# SAHARA product scope and go-to-market readiness

## Shipped in this ZIP
Identity and roles; resident SOS and assistance requests; opt-in geolocation; volunteer presence and offer matching; mission acceptance; mission status and verification; basic rewards; dark responsive dashboards; incident map; mission tracking; mission chat with polling.

## Planned / not shipped
Native Android/iOS apps; Nexconn Chat and Call SDK integration; push notifications and offline delivery; AI dispatch decision support; audited multi-volunteer race-safe claiming; route polyline on map; verified responders; emergency-service integration; fraud detection; organization tenancy; accessibility certification; privacy retention controls; service-level guarantees; payments and billing; admin audit trails; incident escalation and automatic reassignment.

## Commercialization gates
1. Security and privacy review, threat model, and consent UX.
2. Database migrations, durable queues, logging, observability, disaster recovery.
3. Verified volunteer identity and incident handling policies.
4. Reliable push and call delivery; 24/7 operational staffing and escalation.
5. Real-time location privacy, tracking background lifecycle, stale location alerts.
6. Provider contracts for routing, maps, messaging and calls.
7. User acceptance, accessibility, load, penetration and field tests.
8. Legal counsel on applicable regulations and liability.

## UI direction
Forest green and ivory for general workspace; dark navy command-map surface, teal responder markers, red SOS markers, amber pending states, large rounded cards and modern typography.
